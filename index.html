FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need to get started?

A MetaTrader 5 (MT5) trading account. Every plan covers one MT5 account. Request your trial and we'll help you get set up on Telegram.

How do I buy a plan?

Tap the button on the plan you want. Telegram opens with a message ready to send. Send it and we'll reply with payment details and setup help. See every step on our How to Buy page.

How does the free trial work?

Once your request is approved you get a 5-day EA license for one MT5 account. It's a way to get familiar with the strategy before you choose a plan.

What's the difference between Elite and Diamond?

Elite ($5/month) is for traders who open their broker account through our referral, and includes Telegram bot access. Diamond ($49/month) has no referral requirement, so you can keep your own broker, and includes one basic VPS while your plan is paid.

How are trades closed?

When you open a trade, the app runs it for a fixed period and closes it automatically. You can also take profit by closing the basket on your holding.

Is profit guaranteed?

No. Trading gold involves real risk, and past results don't guarantee future returns. Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Read our Risk Disclosure.
