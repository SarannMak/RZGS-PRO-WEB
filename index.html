FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need to get started?

A MetaTrader 5 (MT5) hedging account that can trade gold (XAUUSD). Every plan covers one MT5 account. Claim your trial and we'll help you get set up on Telegram.

How do I buy a plan?

Tap the button on the plan you want. Its steps open right on this website: your broker account (Free and Elite), your MT5 account number, payment, then your bot file. See every step on our Tutorial page.

Do I need a website account?

Yes. Your free website account is where you choose your plan, pay and download your bot file. Your dashboard also shows your plan and its end date.

How does the free trial work?

Open your MT5 account with one of our partner brokers, then claim the trial on this website. Once your account is checked you get a 7-day license for that account. It's a way to get familiar with the strategy before you choose a plan.

What's the difference between Elite and VIP?

Elite ($5/month, or $45 for a year) is for traders who open their broker account with one of our partner brokers. VIP ($29/month, or $289 for a year) works at any broker and unlocks VIP mode in the control app: when a first trade reaches a small profit, a short countdown starts and the trade closes if it is still in profit. Both include the phone control app.

How are trades closed?

A single trade closes at its take profit. When a side holds two or more trades, they close together when their total profit reaches the basket target. You can also close a side, or everything, from the control app.

Is profit guaranteed?

No. Trading gold involves real risk, and past results don't guarantee future returns. Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Read our Risk Disclosure.
