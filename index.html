FAQ

Questions, answered

What do I need to get started?

A MetaTrader 5 (MT5) hedging account that can trade gold (XAUUSD). Every plan covers one MT5 account. Request your trial and we'll help you get set up on Telegram.

How do I buy a plan?

Tap the button on the plan you want. Our Telegram bot opens with that plan already chosen and guides you step by step: broker account, payment, MT5 account number, then your bot file. See every step on our How to Buy page.

Do I need a website account?

No. You can get and buy the bot with Telegram alone. A free website account gives you a dashboard that shows your plan and its end date. Connect it with one tap on the dashboard.

How does the free trial work?

Open your MT5 account with one of our partner brokers, then claim the trial in our Telegram bot. Once it is approved you get a 7-day EA license for that account. It's a way to get familiar with the strategy before you choose a plan.

What's the difference between Elite and Diamond?

Elite ($5/month) is for traders who open their broker account through our referral, and includes the phone control app. Diamond ($49/month) has no referral requirement, so you can keep your own broker, and includes one basic VPS while your plan is paid.

How are trades closed?

A single trade closes at its take profit. When a side holds two or more trades, they close together when their total profit reaches the basket target. You can also close a side, or everything, from the control app.

Is profit guaranteed?

No. Trading gold involves real risk, and past results don't guarantee future returns. Only trade with money you can afford to lose. Read our Risk Disclosure.
