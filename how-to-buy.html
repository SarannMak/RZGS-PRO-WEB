Before you start

What you'll need

An MT5 trading account

It has to be a hedging account. Diamond works with any broker. For the free trial and Elite, open it with one of our partner brokers.

A place to run MT5

Your own computer, or a VPS so the bot can run 24/7. Diamond includes a basic VPS.

A website account

Your plan, your payment and your bot file are all on your dashboard. Setup help and support are on Telegram if you need them.

After you buy

Renewing

You can renew or start another plan once your plan has ended: choose it on your dashboard. The bot stops when the plan ends, so check any open trades first. Don't want to wait? You can cancel your plan on your dashboard, but the days you have left are not refunded and don't move to the new plan.

Changing MT5 account

Need to move your license to another MT5 account? Message us and we'll help. See the License Rules.
