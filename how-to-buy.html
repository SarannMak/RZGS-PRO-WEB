Step by step

How buying works

  1. 1

    Choose your plan

    Start with the free 5-day trial, or pick Elite or Diamond.

    See plans
  2. 2

    Tap the plan button

    Telegram opens with a message already written for you. Just tap Send.

  3. 3

    Share your details

    Tell us your MT5 account number and your broker. On Elite, we'll first send you our referral link so you can open your account with it.

  4. 4

    Make your payment

    We'll send you the payment details on Telegram. Your license is activated once your payment is confirmed. The free trial needs no payment.

  5. 5

    You'll receive the EA and a license for your MT5 account. We'll guide you through the installation, and help set up your VPS if you're on Diamond.

  6. 6

    Start AI trading

    Select the asset and your trade amount, pick an AI model and tap the AI Trading button. That's it.
