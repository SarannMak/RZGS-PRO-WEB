1. Who we are

This policy explains how RZGS-PRO, operated by [your legal business name] ("we", "us"), collects and uses your information when you visit our website, request a free trial or buy a plan.

2. Information we collect

  • Telegram details: your Telegram username and the messages you send us.
  • Trading account details: your MT5 account number and broker name, so we can link your license to your account.
  • Payment details: the information we need to confirm your payment, such as a transaction reference.
  • Email: your email address, if you contact us by email.

3. How we use it

  • To activate and manage your license.
  • To give you setup help and support.
  • To send you renewal reminders and important notices.
  • To prevent misuse, such as repeated free trials or shared licenses.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We only share it when we need to in order to provide the service, for example:

  • with our VPS provider, if you're on the Diamond plan;
  • with payment providers, to confirm your payment;
  • with our partner broker, to confirm that an Elite account was opened through our referral.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

5. This website

This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google may receive your IP address when you visit. When you tap a Telegram button, Telegram's own privacy policy applies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your license is active, and afterwards only for as long as we need it for legal, accounting or security reasons.

7. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No way of storing or sending information is completely secure, but we work to keep yours safe.

8. Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by contacting us. We may need to keep some information for legal reasons.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Message us on Telegram or email [your email]. See all the ways to reach us on our Contact page.
