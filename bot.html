Meet the bot

An automated trading bot built for gold

RZGS-PRO is an Expert Advisor (EA) for MetaTrader 5. It trades gold (XAUUSD) with fixed rules: it opens a buy and a sell together, takes profit on one side, and manages the other side with a grid and a basket close.

How it trades

From first pair to closed basket

Once the bot is active, every cycle follows the same rules. The bot does not predict the price.

  1. 01

    Open a pair

    The bot opens a buy and a sell on gold at the same time. Each one has its own take profit.

  2. 02

    Take profit

    When the price moves, one side reaches its take profit and closes. The other side stays open.

  3. 03

    Add the next pair

    If the price keeps going by the grid distance, the bot opens the next pair. The lot of the side that is holding grows step by step.

  4. 04

    Close & cool down

    When a side holds two or more trades, they close together at the basket target. When nothing is open, the bot cools down, then starts a new cycle.

What's inside

The control app, tab by tab

You run the bot from your phone. Five tabs, one screen at a time.

Core: live status

See if the bot is active, paused or in cooldown, which grid level it is on, the risk level and the next entry price. Balance, equity, today's profit, floating P/L and drawdown update live.

Trades: grid ladder

A gold chart shows every open buy and sell with its lot, the next entry, and the price where each side closes. Below it are both baskets and the list of open positions.

Control: run the bot

Activate or pause with one tap, or let the running cycle finish. Choose Basic, Smart or High risk lots, add a cooldown, or close buys, sells or everything. Every close asks you to confirm.

Tools: settings, risk and report

See the settings your bot runs with, and adjust take profit, basket profit and the Smart mode grid distance. The risk calculator shows the floating result at each grid level if price keeps moving one way. The report shows today's closed profit, TP hits, baskets and the last 7 days.

Log: alerts from the bot

Every TP hit, basket close, cooldown and risk warning is listed with its time, together with the commands you sent.

A private link to your bot

Log in with your MT5 account number and the app password you set in the bot inputs. In the app you can change that password, see how many devices are logged in and log the others out.
