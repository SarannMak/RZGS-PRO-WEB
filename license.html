The full rules

In more detail

1. Your license

When you get a plan, you receive a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the RZGS-PRO EA on one MT5 trading account for the length of your plan. You're licensing the software, not buying it.

2. Free trial

One free trial per person. The 5 days start when your trial is approved. We may decline or end a trial if these rules are misused.

3. Elite and our referral broker

Elite is only for MT5 accounts opened through our broker referral link. If your account is no longer under our referral, your Elite license may stop working. You can switch to Diamond to keep using the bot with any broker.

4. Diamond and the VPS

Diamond works with any broker and includes one basic VPS while your plan is paid. When your plan ends, the VPS is removed, so keep a copy of anything you need from it.

5. Changing your MT5 account

Need to use a different MT5 account? Message us on Telegram. Once we move your license, the bot stops working on the old account.

6. Renewal and expiry

Renew before your license ends to avoid a break. When a license expires, the bot stops working on your account until you renew. Please check and manage any open trades before your license ends.

7. Updates

Bot updates are included while your license is active.

8. Breaking the rules

If a license is shared, resold, copied or tampered with, we may end it immediately without a refund.

9. Trading risk

A license gives you access to software, not guaranteed results. Trading gold carries real risk. Please read our Risk Disclosure.

10. Changes to these rules

We may update these rules from time to time. The latest version is always on this page. Questions about your license? Contact us.
